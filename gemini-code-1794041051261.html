About Me

I am a data analyst with a background in mathematics education and applied statistics. Since 2019 I have worked at the Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education of Indonesia, where I manage large education databases, build ETL pipelines and design dashboards that track national programmes across provinces, school levels and individual schools.

My work sits between data and policy. On a typical project I talk with programme owners to define the right indicators, clean and join data from several systems, build the analysis or dashboard, and then train the people who will use it.

What I Focus On

  • Education Data Products: Dashboards & web apps with role-based access.
  • Data Engineering: ETL pipelines, data quality checks & database consolidation.
  • Statistical Modelling: Program evaluation, SEM/GSCA & forecasting.
  • Data Literacy: Training stakeholders & writing standard operating procedures (SOP).
