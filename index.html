About Me

5+ years in education data

I am a data analyst with a background in mathematics education and applied statistics. Since 2019, I have worked at the Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education of Indonesia (Kemendikdasmen), managing large-scale education databases, building ETL pipelines, and designing interactive dashboards that track national programmes across provinces, school levels, and individual schools.

My work sits right at the intersection between data engineering, statistical rigor, and public policy. On typical projects, I collaborate with programme owners to define key indicators, consolidate data from fragmented systems, build reproducible pipelines, and deliver intuitive visual analytics.

Beyond monitoring dashboards, I leverage statistical modeling (regression, structural equation approaches, time-series forecasting) to evaluate programme outcomes. My research on school accreditation and national assessment results is published in peer-reviewed journals.

National Education Priority Programme Dashboard
The Problem

Leadership had to open multiple separate spreadsheets to check priority program progress. Figures from different systems returned conflicting results due to ununified data filters.

What I Did
  • Defined standardized key indicators (teacher-student ratio, class ratios, province progress).
  • Built an R Shiny dashboard (bs4Dash) with KPI cards and interactive filter tables.
  • Implemented role-based security & access scoping per province.
  • Containerized app deployment using Docker & Nginx reverse proxy on Linux VPS.
Tech Stack

R · Shiny · bs4Dash · DT · SQL · Docker · Nginx

School Quality Risk Dashboard (SPMI)
The Problem

Quality assurance data for over 12,000 schools was available, but officers lacked a fast way to pinpoint at-risk schools or weakest national standards.

What I Did
  • Authored Product Requirements Document (PRD) and user access matrix.
  • Designed a risk scoring formula combining average standard scores and variance gaps.
  • Built summary drill-down UI leading to school profile level.
Tech Stack

Google Apps Script · Google Sheets · HTML/JS · PRD Design
